    Measurements are valid only for points lying on the sheet's flat surface. Anything raised above the paper, such as the arch or the top of the foot, reads larger than it is. A traced outline photographed on the sheet avoids this. Lens distortion is not corrected, so keep the camera square-on and a little way back. Check any new set-up by measuring the 100 mm scale bar printed on the sheet. This tool is for measurement support and is not a validated clinical device.
